## Nutrify 3D exercise illustrations (1.2.2)
Created using OpenAI image generation for Nutrify. These are illustrative still images, not motion capture or a measurement of muscle activation. Production images are bundled locally as WebP without changing their composition. Full generation prompts are retained in `src/data/exercise-art-prompts.json`.

The flat barbell bench press (assets/exercises-3d/73.webp) is an AI-assisted visual adaptation of the two Everkinetic illustrations distributed by wger: https://wger.de/en/exercise/73/view/ and source URLs in src/data/exercise-details.json. Original creator: Everkinetic. Changes: 3D rendering, color, lighting, background and composition of the two positions. This derivative image is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Other newly generated exercise illustrations are generated from exercise-specific text descriptions, without reference-image inputs. Existing wger images and instructions keep their original licenses.

App muscle mappings were corrected for hip adduction, oblique crunch, reverse curl, dumbbell shrug and kettlebell swing. This does not change source attribution for the original instructional text.
